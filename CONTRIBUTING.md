# Contributing to Immortal Inference

Thank you for your interest in contributing to `immortal-inference`!

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

To protect the project, ensure clean copyright ownership, and maintain our dual-licensing structure, all individual contributors must sign our Contributor License Agreement (CLA) prior to having their pull requests merged.

* When you open a Pull Request, our automated **CLA Assistant** workflow will prompt you to sign the agreement directly within GitHub.
* By submitting a contribution, you agree to assign/grant necessary rights to **TheImmortalPython Labs** to maintain, distribute, and dual-license the codebase.

## Code Standards

* Ensure all code adheres to clean architectural standards, includes appropriate unit tests, and maintains high performance for inference pipelines regardless of the implementation language (Python, Rust, C++, etc.).
* Respect proper diacritics for Sanskrit terminology in documentation, comments, and theoretical specifications.
