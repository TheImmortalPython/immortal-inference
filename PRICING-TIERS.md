# Enterprise Pricing & Commercial Licensing Strategy

**Version 1.0 — TheImmortalPython Labs / Joshua St. Germain**

## 1. Overview and Purpose

This document outlines the commercial pricing tiers for `immortal-inference`. Because the foundational software is published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3), any commercial entity deploying the codebase over a network to provide proprietary services must either open-source their derivative infrastructure under the AGPLv3 or acquire a Commercial Exception License.

All commercial licenses and software assets offered under this strategy are conveyed **strictly on an as-is basis as self-service intellectual property acquisitions**. Transactions convey direct software rights, codebase access, and AGPLv3 network copyleft exemptions only. They do not include ongoing consulting services, custom engineering implementations, software-level support desks, SLAs, or managed engineering deployments.

## 2. Commercial Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Mid-Market Commercial License

* **Target Audience:** Mid-market SaaS providers and Series B+ startups embedding the engine within internal or customer-facing production environments.
* **Legal Trigger:** Private network deployment of `immortal-inference` without AGPLv3 source-disclosure requirements.
* **Pricing Model:** Flat annual software acquisition fee (self-service; strictly as-is).
* **Rate:** $25,000 to $100,000 per year, scaled by company headcount and active staging/production environments.

### Tier 2: Token-Volume / Inference Capacity License

* **Target Audience:** High-scale AI infrastructure providers, hyper-scalers, and API gateway operators.
* **Legal Trigger:** Centralized cloud inference pipelines processing high-volume token streams through the deterministic containment engine.
* **Pricing Model:** Tiered self-service capacity licensing based on active token throughput or GPU cluster footprint.
* **Rate:** Baseline of $0.005 per 1,000,000 processed tokens or $0.25 per active GPU compute hour, scaled dynamically based on aggregate monthly volume.

### Tier 3: Gross Revenue Share / Commercial Royalty

* **Target Audience:** Companies embedding the engine into flagship, high-margin commercial AI applications as a primary value driver.
* **Legal Trigger:** Utilization of the core containment engine to secure core application functionality and commercial revenue streams.
* **Pricing Model:** Top-line commercial royalty override.
* **Rate:** 1.5% to 3.0% of gross revenue generated by the application utilizing the engine.

### Tier 4: Uncapped Enterprise "Safe Harbor" Site License

* **Target Audience:** Tech conglomerates and major cloud platforms requiring enterprise-wide coverage without individual developer or project tracking.
* **Legal Trigger:** Enterprise-wide integration across multiple internal engineering groups and global product lines.
* **Pricing Model:** Comprehensive organizational IP conveyance and safe-harbor release.
* **Rate:** $500,000 to $5,000,000+ per year, customized to total annual corporate revenue and employee scale.

## 3. Terms of Delivery & Compliance

* **As-Is IP Conveyance:** All software assets, source code repositories, and commercial exception grants are provided strictly on an as-is basis. TheImmortalPython Labs maintains zero obligation to provide custom engineering, deployment assistance, dedicated support channels, or service-level agreements (SLAs).
* **Self-Reporting & Audit:** Commercial licensees operating under Tiers 3 and 4 agree to provide annual certified self-reporting of gross revenue and total deployment scale upon request to maintain valid safe-harbor status under their commercial exception agreements.
