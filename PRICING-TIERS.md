# Enterprise Pricing & Commercial Licensing Strategy

**Version 1.0 — TheImmortalPython Labs / Joshua St. Germain**

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document outlines the commercial pricing tiers for immortal-inference. Because the software is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3), any corporate entity operating the code over a network to provide commercial services must either open-source their entire surrounding stack or acquire a commercial exception license. This pricing framework establishes structured pathways for mid-market and enterprise entities to secure commercial compliance.

## 2. Commercial Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Mid-Market Commercial License
- **Target Audience:** Mid-market SaaS companies and Series B startups.
- **Legal Trigger:** Private network deployment of immortal-inference within internal or customer-facing production environments.
- **Pricing Model:** Flat annual fee combined with standard software SLAs.
- **Rate:** $25,000 to $100,000 per year, scaled by company headcount and staging environments.

### Tier 2: Token-Volume / Inference Capacity Tax
- **Target Audience:** High-scale AI infrastructure providers and API gateway operators.
- **Legal Trigger:** Centralized cloud inference pipelines processing high-volume token streams through the deterministic containment engine.
- **Pricing Model:** Tiered production scaling based on active token throughput or GPU cluster capacity.
- **Rate:** Baseline of $0.005 per 1,000,000 processed tokens or $0.25 per active GPU compute hour, scaled dynamically based on aggregate monthly volume.

### Tier 3: Gross Revenue Share / Commercial Royalty
- **Target Audience:** Companies embedding the engine into flagship, high-margin commercial AI applications.
- **Legal Trigger:** Utilization of the core engine to secure core application revenue streams.
- **Pricing Model:** Top-line business result override.
- **Rate:** 1.5% to 3% of gross revenue generated by the application utilizing the engine.

### Tier 4: Uncapped Enterprise "Safe Harbor" Site License
- **Target Audience:** Large tech conglomerates and major cloud providers (e.g., Google, AWS, Microsoft, OpenAI).
- **Legal Trigger:** Enterprise-wide deployment across multiple internal engineering groups without individual developer tracking.
- **Pricing Model:** Comprehensive organizational coverage.
- **Rate:** $500,000 to $5,000,000+ per year, customized to total annual corporate revenue and employee count.

## 3. Audit and Compliance
Commercial licensees operating under Tiers 3 and 4 agree to provide annual certified self-reporting of gross revenue and total deployment scale upon request to maintain compliance under commercial exception agreements.
