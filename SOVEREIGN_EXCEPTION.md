# Sovereign Infrastructure & Public Sector License Exception

**Version 1.0 — TheImmortalPython Labs / Joshua St. Germain**

## 1. Purpose and Intent

This Exception is established to protect and secure national critical infrastructure, public research, and democratic governance systems. It provides a legal "Safe Harbor" for qualifying Public Sector Entities to utilize, modify, and host `immortal-inference` within private or secure networks without triggering the viral source-code disclosure obligations mandated under Section 13 of the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3).

## 2. Qualifying Entities

An organization automatically qualifies for this Sovereign Exception if they are a:

* (a) Federal, state, or municipal government agency or department;
* (b) Publicly funded academic or scientific research institution;
* (c) Certified operator of National Critical Infrastructure (including, but not limited to, nuclear facilities, electrical power grids, civil aviation control, and public healthcare networks).

## 3. Granted Exceptions

Qualifying Entities are hereby granted a royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual license to operate this software over a computer network to provide remote services (SaaS, API gateways, or model inference pipelines) without any obligation to disclose, publish, or open-source their surrounding infrastructure, application code, or weights under Section 13 of the AGPLv3.

## 4. Conditions and Exclusions

* **No Cloud Commercialization:** This exception strictly DOES NOT apply if a Qualifying Entity packages this software to sell a commercial, general-purpose public cloud API competing with the Author's commercial enterprise offerings.
* **Attribution:** The original copyright notices in the source files must remain intact.

## 6. Geopolitical Compliance & Export Control Restrictions

This Sovereign Exception strictly DOES NOT apply to, and explicitly revokes all non-AGPL usage rights from:

* (a) Any entity, government, or infrastructure operator currently designated on the United States Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Sanctions Lists;
* (b) Any nation-state or state-sponsored actor subject to active comprehensive US export controls or embargoes.

Entities operating in restricted territories must strictly adhere to the default, unmodified terms of the public AGPLv3 license, including full source-code disclosure of all network-interacting architectures, without exception.
